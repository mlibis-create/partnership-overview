YT
YouTube
Shorts hook. Long form converts. Full funnel, one ecosystem.
Sales narrative
"YouTube treats creators as premium media — not social posts."
YouTube's philosophy maps to full-funnel: Shorts drive discovery and awareness, long-form deepens loyalty and converts. No other platform can do both inside one creator ecosystem. When pitching YouTube reps, speak in IMA, GCS/LCS, MRP, and Shorts. They're measured on creator-led ad spend and media investment. The hook: #paid is part of an exclusive network of vetted creator marketing agencies YouTube has established to bridge the gap between ad sales capabilities and high-performing, creator-led Shorts content.

Creator-led vs. brand-led: Use creators to drive discovery — brief the objective, let them find the hook. Best for awareness, engagement, and Partnership Ads. Brand-led is for demos, how-tos, and consistent voice over time. The best strategies use both.

P
Pinterest
Discovery-first. Evergreen by design.
Sales narrative
"Pinterest isn't social media — it's a search and discovery engine where creators have influence without chasing it."
Pinterest users go there to find ideas and discover brands — not to be entertained or earn likes. The #1 reason people use Pinterest is to find information about products and brands. Creator content on Pinterest helps audiences discover brands in authentic and relatable ways; ads blend in seamlessly and provide ongoing, evergreen performance.

What differentiates creators on Pinterest from other platforms: the most influential creators on Pinterest aren't in it for the influence. Expertise, trends, and finding ideas outperform personality-driven content. This is why creator content here compounds over time — it doesn't expire with the news cycle.

#paid's unique advantage: 30-day delivery (current partners take 8–10 weeks), Canadian market expertise, curated creator handraise process that speeds up activation, and consistent thought leadership that positions us as a strategic partner — not just an executor.

M
Meta
Creative diversification. Partnership Ads. MPA.
Sales narrative
"Your clients can't scale spend without scaling creative — and creator content is the most efficient path."
Meta's ad performance problem is a creative diversification problem. Most brands run 3–5 ad variants per campaign. Advantage+ needs volume and variety to optimize. Without it, creative fatigue sets in within 2–3 weeks, CPAs rise, and spend stalls. Creator-powered content solves this: 5 creators × 5–6 assets each = 25–30 unique creative assets in 30 days. More creative inputs → better algorithm optimization → lower CPA → scalable spend.

The vocabulary that lands with Meta reps: Creative Diversification, Partnership Ads, MPA, JBP, Advantage+. Meta reps are measured on media spend and Partnership Ad activation. Frame the pitch around what unlocks their metrics, not ours.

The MPA is the foot in the door — but don't be limited by it. Even clients who aren't eligible for MPA are ones Meta reps want spending on Partnership Ads. Position greater creator content volume as the best path to Partnership Ad spend.

S
Snap
Real Friends, Real Influence — closeness over reach.
Sales narrative
"Snap treats creators as trusted amplifiers — not media placements."
Snap's creator philosophy is rooted in closeness and authenticity. Unlike reach-first platforms, Snap believes creators drive value through real relationships with tight-knit audiences — not follower counts. Snap Stars are vetted for their ability to create Stories and Snap content that feels native, personal, and credible to their specific community.

Quick stats that land with Snap reps: 946M monthly active users globally. 3.5B unique friend pairs snapping daily. Users open Snapchat 40x per day. Top creators post 140x/day across Stories and Spotlight. 100K+ Snap Creators available globally for brand partnerships.

Stories vs. Spotlight: A creator Story post is for brands that want organic traction with the creator's followers. A Spotlight post is for brands wanting to reach beyond the creator's followers — wider audience, more discovery-oriented.
